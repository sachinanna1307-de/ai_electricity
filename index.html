💡 Energy Saving Expert Tips

AI-recommended actions to optimize power consumption for major household appliances.

❄️ Air Conditioner (AC)

Set temperature to 24°C instead of 18°C. Saves up to 24% electricity per month.

🌀 Ceiling Fans

Turn off fans when leaving rooms. Use BLDC energy-efficient fans to reduce usage by 50%.

🧊 Refrigerator

Keep fridge away from direct sunlight and hot appliances. Maintain temperature at 3°C to 4°C.

💡 Lighting & Bulbs

Replace conventional CFL/tubes with certified LED bulbs. Clean lamp shades regularly.
